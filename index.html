Azat Rakhmanberdiev

Political Science · Heidelberg University
Research Assistant · GESIS / European Social Survey

I am a master's student in political science at Heidelberg University and a research assistant with the European Social Survey (ESS) Team at GESIS – Leibniz Institute for the Social Sciences.

My research focuses on American political behavior, political polarization, political psychology, and survey methodology, with particular attention to measurement and construct validity.

Manuscripts

Violent Political Behavior in the United States: Measurement, Ideological Composition, and Dynamics

Azat Rakhmanberdiev · 2026 · Under review at Political Studies

preprint ↗figure ↓
abstract / research overview

This study develops a broader measure of realized violent political behavior and examines its ideological composition and temporal dynamics using ACLED event data from 2020 to 2025. The analysis combines original event coding and a recurrent-event temporal exposure design.

Religiosity or Religious Identity? Measuring Muslim Identity in the Study of Anti-Americanism

Azat Rakhmanberdiev · 2026

preprint ↗
abstract / research overview

Using two waves of Arab Barometer data for Iraq, this study examines the conceptual and statistical limitations of existing measures of religious identity and assesses the sensitivity of substantive conclusions to alternative operationalizations and sample restrictions.
